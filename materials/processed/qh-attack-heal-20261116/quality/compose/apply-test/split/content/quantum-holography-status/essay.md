# Ordered composition and independent comparisons

The Heisenberg current gives a basic example. Its singular product has a double pole with coefficient equal to the level times the vacuum. A unital field map to scalars would have to preserve that pole. Scalar fields have no singular products. The current algebra therefore has a vacuum but no scalar augmentation at nonzero level.

Associative chiral Koszul duality retains this distinction. It studies multiplication, modules, and singular collision with the order of the inputs fixed. A complex on configurations records the loci where operators meet. A field algebra records the corresponding coefficients. A module category records actions and fusion. A comparison must specify the maps between these objects and the operations they preserve.

Ordinary chiral operations on a smooth curve impose a strong restriction. Strict associativity forces both triple composites to vanish. A local vacuum that normalizes one side then forces the whole module to vanish. Coherent associative constructions require a larger coefficient system. The Lie–commutative theory supplies enveloping examples, while the general associative field comparison remains a separate problem.

Ordered configurations have collision maps and cuts into consecutive blocks. A residue retains the principal part and its normal derivatives. A cut gives a map from the whole configuration to its blocks. In the connected augmented coefficient category, bar and cobar are inverse equivalences. A reduced word at n positions has length at most n. This finite arity filtration does not remove the need to specify mode completion or continuous duality.

For identity exchange, a separate symmetric algebraic diagram now supplies all-surjection counits, the product, full-complex homotopies and an additive arity splitting. Its vacuum tangent is central and square-zero. The scalar and free-translation formulas retain their specific inputs. General exchange, the comparison with the non-separated atlas and continuous curved duality remain additional problems; the new diagram is not an automatic equivalence of all those recipients.

The empty convolution unit and the local vacuum have different supports. The diagonal tensor has its own dualizing unit. Atlas-to-word restriction can discard a nonzero separated summand. Curvature records the failure of a chosen scalar splitting to preserve multiplication. These data remain part of the duality even when an underlying complex or its cohomology forgets them.

Several field realizations are explicit. They include polynomial free currents, curved universal quadratic-linear-scalar algebras, marked charged jets, and paired Gaussian surface fields. Native rank-two matching exists at the first quantum precision, while polar fields exist at the next precision. The remaining native comparison must identify composite marks, actual evaluation, both rooted actions, and every required collision square.

The forward coefficient problem shows why a nonzero extension class is insufficient. An enlarged jet quotient retains the actual class and global regular products. Its derived vacuum branches also exist. Fixed singleton types can still force an occupied collision to vanish, and fixed products can retain the wrong curvature. A successful successor must change the permitted domain or category while preserving the original markings through an explicit comparison.

The transverse geometry has positive comparisons at several levels. Analytic configuration functions, finite-pole conormal coefficients and native ray evaluations retain their established domains. Independent rooted forests now have actual relative operations with both occupied vacua. The unrestricted scalar current complex is now computed with three escaping summands in addition to the two forests. Their normal coordinates act invertibly, while the forest sum is characterized by simultaneous normal local nilpotence. The scalar vacuum evaluation has exactly that forest image and the escaping cokernel, so its old global quasi-isomorphism assertion is false. A successful native extension must carry this calculation to state coefficients and all collision maps while retaining the marked normal torsion. Adding a uniform normal-order bound changes the problem. A separate three-position construction now uses two actual contact images to form a cofiber and obtain the marked normal-linear comparison at invertible level. It preserves the full old first cohomology and removes only the two level-weighted forest modules from second cohomology. Both polar vacua and the nonsplit extension survive, but the comparison kills a nonzero source class and has no homotopy left inverse. Independent all-position native origin, completed domains and the unscaled zero-inclusive map remain open.

Affine bulk cochains have algebraic state operations with the prescribed product and shifted secondary pairing. The full two-copy projected fields agree on all algebraic states. At invertible shifted form, the ghost-jet secondary is the average of the endpoint extractions. A geometric bulk comparison must also construct supported operation complexes, boundary actions, and their collision homotopies.

The available centres solve different representing problems. Convolution centres and rooted vacuum endomorphisms exist. Two-colour actions now have rooted collision homotopies, compatible pure-wall nullity and common-winding vanishing. A constant singleton with the specified global propagating insertion also has a mixed-source binary filler retaining one prescribed cut and both collision branches. The exterior example still has a two-dimensional family of final fillers. Uniform-source data, comparisons between independent cuts and coherent higher choices remain before full recognition. Algebraic nonrepresentability does not decide every physically specified action space. In the separate Heisenberg base-change problem, surjective base change is now equivalent to the full tame cocycle subcategory defined by an acyclic generated ideal. The full inclusion remains open, including higher homotopy. A functorial incoming tame refinement would suffice; relative PBW is one possible route. This partial result does not establish the full localized-action comparison.

Filtered vacuum composition now has an all-level ambient model with its opposite order retained. The shifted filtration controls repeated zero-energy ghosts. An explicit all-arity map compares the bar and Chevalley models, and signed transposition gives the contragredient vacuum corner. Ordinary composition changes to the complementary level. The natural finite-energy exact category misses ambient third extensions, so it cannot supply every intrinsic comparison.

The reflected Virasoro comparison is available at every complex charge on the full subcategory of bounded-below smooth complexes with jointly nilpotent cohomology. Its augmentation is the restricted contragredient vacuum, and its ordinary endomorphism algebra is formal exterior on one degree-three class even at nonsimple charges. The actual vacuum-to-contragredient map gives a multiplicative endomorphism comparison with a nonzero cone at nonsimple charges; at simple charges the normalized map is invertible. Matching these two marks does not determine the full unbounded null class, native collision functor or physical state.

Algebraic collision coherence extends beyond individual associators. The fixed-contraction rational gauge retains its literal four-position pentagon. Horizontal lifting, fixed-deletion strict units, finite subdivision comparisons and full-bar permutohedral evaluation families act on a faithful regular module. An explicit algebraic cobar map is a retraction of shuffle, and the other composite has a relative polynomial algebra homotopy to the identity. These operations are finite on each algebraic input. Strict coalgebra retraction and arbitrary completed-dual comparison remain different questions. A native realization must still match the actual vacuum and endpoint operations, tensorator, connection and higher faces.

Line fusion depends on the module domain. Finite Weyl sectors have explicit cochain, counit, exchange, and associator comparisons. Ordered completed Heisenberg products preserve the admitted quasi-isomorphisms through two recursive completion levels. Fixed finite trees retain exactness on their admitted inputs. General third-level strictness, intrinsic leg recovery, common chamber transport, and geometric module units remain open.

The abelian physical example fixes a variance issue. State transport and operation transport are inverse transposes, so their specified loop phases differ. A physical endpoint functor must use the corresponding opposite transport or reverse braiding. The coefficient-native singleton category retains bounded composition, units and finite duals. Its ordinary-derived point kernel is now constructed as local cohomology at zero charge, while a separate hybrid diagonal coring retains all algebraic charged complexes. Finite separated higher Wick maps and chosen interior primitive transport also exist. These constructions keep their distinct tensor and weak-equivalence categories. The full geometric cut actor, common completion, nonsplit left endpoint and physical sewing still require their own comparisons.

Rational, multiplicative, elliptic and monodromic coefficients impose different analytic requirements. The elliptic branch now has an actual local/formal cubic state-field map on all algebraic polynomial jets, with the original midpoint lower fields and exact admitted all-state exchange. The four-factor argument and uniform generating collision bound supply locality and weak associativity. Exact midpoint-to-endpoint transport and admitted nonlinear biderivation corrections preserve that finite-precision construction. A common global coefficient category, native supported operations, marked KZ and associator comparisons, and quartic or all-order extensions remain separate.

Configuration arity and deformation order are independent. An all-finite family of collision homotopies does not give the fifth coefficient of a quantum exchange. In continuous formality, a quartic obstruction forces higher classical source operations. Proper-domain homotopies remain valid, while the common quantum comparison and its coherent inverse require further construction.

Surface theories provide an all-arity paired Gaussian realization and a precise native cubic criterion. For full retained-point morphisms, the unary Lie map must preserve the central cubic tensor. This criterion includes collisions, vacancies, permutations, and mixed coherences. It does not construct the independent interacting bulk or classify all automorphisms.

Free elliptic reduction has confined cubic current limits and an explicit central de Rham primitive. Integration by parts retains distributional limits where high compact-anchor jets destroy absolute moments. The specified incoming relation rows now admit arbitrary finite holomorphic right jets, after the required third-pair derivative transfer. Marked all-top and bare-eta rows retain their actual source equations and joint faces. The canonical cubic cone and contraction are available. The full relative map still needs broader history words, the compact endpoint and independently prescribed projected, contact, localized and harmonic arrows.

Gravity requires analytic estimates on an actual operation diagram. The ordinary cubic-field operation now has two independent holomorphic jets and a differential-compatible square on the stated first-jet source. Selected first-normal comparisons retain separate bare-relative and complete-endpoint limits, and specified tangential overlaps are uniform. Coupled level variation must differentiate the whole constructed family. These results do not give separate limits for each endpoint summand. The full analytic admissible class and its correction quotient, all normal operations on that source, independent-height-ratio collar, higher compositions and physical stress comparison remain to be constructed.

Continuous Virasoro sewing retains the adopted local theorem above charge one and the geometric tree theorem at fixed charge above twenty-five. A later construction now admits four completed inputs on a common entire-energy space over the five collar strips. Its actual flip identities and the independent label pentagon give collar coherence under the retained boundary hypothesis. Typed spectral associators have inverses on the same output energy scales. Full integer-mode Ward covariance and both rapid completed-vacuum placements also hold on their stated domains. A resolved nested norm retains its intermediate multiplicity label; integrating that label is a different operation. Point-label evaluation, collision-closed input domains, a Ward universal property, all unit triangles and general interior or higher-arity sewing remain open.

A separate one-screening torus family now identifies the actual positive partner measure with its complete Bergman Hilbert space and exact continuous kernel. The corrected proof uses a uniform integrable inverse-square-root height bound; the older unweighted domination claim is false. With the fixed-cocycle index theorem and the prescribed torus-stack normalization, the exact trace follows. In the original real-external-momentum domain this establishes only \(c=33\), \(P_0=0\), \(h_0=4/3\), with trace \(5/144\). The current generic route still imports the all-level torus AGT representation and assumes actual positivity, regularity, weighted regulator control and an independently justified kernel-covariance input; these hypotheses remain open here. The generic sphere, c25/c28 metric comparison and physical gravitational-state construction remain separate.

The wider programme retains independent Hall, brane, and higher-dimensional problems. Hall products, localized coproducts, and stable envelopes need comparison on one oriented coefficient theory. Numerical Igusa formulas do not determine primitive brackets or cyclic states. Pure-open obstructions coexist with positive cotangent and open–closed theories. A defect comparison must carry the actual quantum obstruction into the independently defined defect operations.

The arithmetic branch has finite-window positivity for the complete Weil form and integral normal/Frobenius constructions. Global positivity requires all compact windows. An arithmetic field realization requires the full trace identity, both endpoint actions, normal collisions, and every prime-power contribution. Finite positive matrices and altered positive damping do not supply those maps.

An independently prepared gravitational state is a further construction. Its amplitudes must select the boundary representation, normalization, observable action, and sewing domain. A character, determinant norm, or algebraic cap does not fix all this data. The comparison must begin with the bulk amplitude and retain its boundary conditions and counterterm prescription.
