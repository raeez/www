# The nine fixed rows in the positive affine calculation

The nine-row prescription passes for the original \(a_+=h_{3,0}\) cocycle. It gives a rank-nine reduction of the previously verified 31-by-41 system. It uses the existing ordinary fixed-fibre formality theorem, and the nine native inhomogeneous values remain unevaluated. Keep normalized Hochschild cochains of positive arity and their complete arity filtration. In the coefficient proof, the internal cochain projection and inclusion split at each arity. Its tensor-Hom contraction \(K\) obeys \(K^2=K\iota=\rho K=0\), since \(S^2=SP=PS=0\), and the corresponding tensor contraction has the same side conditions. A single binary Hochschild perturbation \(\delta\) raises arity. Thus \(D=\rho\delta(1+K\delta)^{-1}\iota\) is the small differential. At fixed output arity this series is finite. Expanding the expression by starting with one homogeneous \(g_n\) gives precisely the homogeneous part of the native 31-by-41 system. The chosen Cartan calibration changes its fixed inhomogeneous term. In particular \(D\)'s part increasing arity by one is the ordinary signed binary differential of the marked cohomology algebra.

I checked the inference from formality to a *total* rank statement. Take a strictly unital, uncurved \(A_\infty\) quasi-isomorphism \(f:H_{\rm formal}\to A\). Compose normalized positive-arity coderivations on the left or the right of its bar coalgebra map. Both resulting maps to the complex of coderivations along \(f\) commute with the differential. Use the full tensor bar for the coalgebra maps, imposing normalization by vanishing on any input unit. If a unit lies outside a Taylor block of the inner operation, strict unitality or the unit condition of the outer operation kills it. The absence of zero-input Taylor terms preserves the positive-arity filtration. On its arity-\(n\) quotient the maps are precomposition with \(f_1^{\otimes n}\) or postcomposition with \(f_1\).

Transfer the three internal complexes before adding the higher-arity terms. In the mixed complex the source \(H\) has zero internal differential, so the output contraction suffices. After perturbation, the two maps between small complexes have invertible diagonal in each arity because \(f_1\) induces a cohomology isomorphism. They have filtration-preserving inverses by induction on arity. Hence the total small native differential between the two specified degrees has the same rank as the formal binary differential. Equality of the ranks of the diagonal blocks alone would not justify this step.

Using an earlier independent implementation of signed Hochschild insertion, I obtained ranks \(3,6,0\) for the \(16\times8,\ 13\times19,\ 2\times14\) binary blocks. The specified \(3\times3\) and \(6\times6\) minors agree entry by entry with the constructor's two matrices, with determinant \(+1\) each. Arity forces the corresponding upper off-diagonal native block to vanish. Thus the full nine-row minor has determinant one, and the preceding filtered equivalence shows its columns span the entire image. The consistent inhomogeneous system can be solved first at the three chosen arity-two rows and then at the six chosen arity-three rows. Every other row follows, including the two arity-four rows. This proves the nine specified measurements suffice for this prescription. An information-theoretic minimum or numerical values for the nine measurements would require additional evidence.
