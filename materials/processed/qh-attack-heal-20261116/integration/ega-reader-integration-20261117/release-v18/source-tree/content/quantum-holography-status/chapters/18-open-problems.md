# The strongest remaining questions

Three reconstruction problems have different targets. A bulk field supported above a boundary disk \(V\) should define an operation on the boundary observables \(\mathcal A(V)\). At a collision, a constructible complex with restriction \(L\) to an open complement \(j:U\hookrightarrow X\) and value \(V_x\) at a closed stratum \(i:\{x\}\hookrightarrow X\) needs an attaching morphism \(V_x\to i^*Rj_*L\). A filling \(M\) assigns a functional \(Z_M\) with values in its amplitude line. One can test a proposed bulk theory by deriving any of these maps and comparing it with an independent construction on the same inputs.

Consider four charge-one rays at level two on the transverse slice \(\mathbb C^3\) to the full diagonal, with \(j\) the inclusion of the distinct-position open and \(L\) the charge-four outgoing coefficient there. The quotients \(P_W=j_!L[3]/i_*W\) by lines \(W\subset H^2(i^*Rj_*L)\cong\mathbb C^2\) give a labelled \(\mathbb P^1\) of attachments with equal stalk and costalk dimensions. At fixed positive half-braid \(+i\), only the trivial and sign lines are \(S_4\)-equivariant. Ordinary inverse image from the coarse quotient selects the quotient by the trivial line within this family. The physical collision must decide whether that descent condition applies. In the disk model of Chapter 11, \(2p_1^2\partial_{p_2}+p_2\partial_{p_1}^2\) has expectations \(+2\) and \(-2\) on \((p_1^2\pm p_2)/2\), whose single-mode laws agree. For the K3 product of Chapter 15 the undetected first-order cubics satisfy \(T(F,-,-)=0\) and \(\tau_qT=0\), where \(F\) is the fibre and \(\tau_q\) contracts with the inverse intersection form. This space has dimension \(1750\).

Composition imposes a further condition on each comparison. If a boundary interface acts by a functor \(F\), its tensor comparison \(F(X)\boxtimes F(Y)\to F(X\boxtimes Y)\) must be defined on the separately prepared inputs and on the intermediate objects produced by a first collision. If a cap assigns a state to a cut, the pairing on a second cut must reproduce the amplitude for the glued filling. The following problems specify those domains and the coefficients on which the compatibility can be tested.
