# One quartic value decides formality with the marked diagram involution

Use the exceptional vacuum algebra, its suspension and the enhanced affine reduction \(i:E\to H\) of [the ternary calculation](AFFINE-TERNARY.md). Assume the genuine diagram involution lifts to the dg data, fixes the deformation parameter and intertwines the marked affine map. Use \(\sigma(a)=a,\sigma(b)=-b,\sigma(c)=c\). The geometric sign calculation for the stated marking gives \(\sigma(u)=-u,\sigma(v)=-v,\sigma(z)=-z\) in eigenvector coordinates. The independent argument for the geometric premise is recorded separately. Over characteristic zero, once equivariance holds, invariant splittings and contractions exist by averaging; the following is a computation in the resulting normalized equivariant bar construction.

**Proposition.** The equivariant minimal \(A_\infty\) algebra can be placed, by an identity-linear gauge, in the form \(b=b_2+b_4\) with exactly one possibly nonzero entry of \(b_4\),
\[
 b_4(sa,sa,sa,su)=\gamma\,s(az).
\]
Equivalently \(\gamma\) is the sum of the \(s(az)\)-coefficients on the four placements of \(su\) among three copies of \(sa\) before this normalizing gauge. It is the suspended all-plus sum; an unsuspended convention requires its own Koszul signs. The ordinary associative dg algebra is formal if and only if \(\gamma=0\) under the stated premises. Neither value is forced by the abstract marked binary algebra and equivariant affine morphism: every \(\gamma\) occurs in a full strictly unital equivariant minimal algebra carrying that same strict affine map.

**Proof.** In these characters all 32 candidate target ternary cells of internal degree \(-1\) transform by \(-1\), so any equivariant minimal ternary vanishes. In particular the two affine-compatible ternary models with \(u\) from the preceding calculation are excluded by this additional symmetry. There are 17 equivariant possible 4-cochain cells of internal degree \(-2\). Their outgoing suspended Hochschild differential to the five degree-compatible arity-five cells has rank four. The incoming differential from the 19 equivariant 3-cochain cells has rank nine. The four classes have representatives
\[
 [aaaa\mid ac],\quad [aaab\mid av],\quad -[aaab\mid z]+[aaa(ab)\mid az],\quad [aaau\mid az].
\]
This list uses the same elementary cochain notation as the ternary calculation. These are four-cochains, independently of the arity of any earlier operation.

At arity four in the \(A_\infty\) morphism from the formal affine exterior source the potential nonlinear comparison term is the target product of two outputs of \(F_2\). Every equivariant relative \(F_2\) can have a nonzero value only on \((b,c),(c,b)\), both with output \(au\). Their pairwise products vanish since \(J^2=0\). Thus the four-point comparison really requires \(b_4i^{\otimes4}\) to lie in the image of the linear relative Hochschild differential on \(F_3\). The latter has ten possible input cochains, thirteen output cells and rank six. Modulo those boundaries the restriction of the four displayed classes has rank three and kernel precisely the last one. No comparison value on \(bc\) or \(abc\) has been discarded from the relative calculation.

The four-word coordinate sum in the proposition annihilates every absolute arity-three gauge coboundary of this internal degree. The corresponding four rows of the incoming matrix have left nullspace the line \((1,1,1,1)\). The displayed representative pairs to one. A potential ordinary identity-linear \(A_\infty\) equivalence between minimal algebras both having zero ternary cannot remove a nonzero class at this next arity. Indeed in the complete bar arity filtration write the identity-linear change as an exponential of a degree-zero coderivation. The arity-three change forces the leading binary generator to commute with \(b_2\); its double commutator therefore vanishes. The arity-four change is then a Hochschild coboundary. The same conclusion for a general linear equivalence follows by composing with its graded-algebra linear part. This proves that the nonzero case obstructs ordinary associative formality.

At arity five the only degree-permitted operations before taking characters are \((a,a,a,a,a)\mapsto av,z\). The source has positive character and both outputs negative, so the equivariant operation vanishes identically. At arity six the smallest possible output degree is \(6\cdot3+2-6=14\), absent in \(H\); increasing a nonunit input raises it to at least sixteen. All greater arities require output above fifteen. Thus once an equivariant arity-three gauge eliminates a cohomologically zero quartic, it can introduce no later operation. This gives formality for \(\gamma=0\). Keeping a single quartic cell \([aaau\mid az]\) gives a full model for any \(\gamma\): its output annihilates the positive ideal and cannot be inserted as one of its own inputs, while \(a,u\) are indecomposable. Its Hochschild boundary and self-insertion both vanish. It vanishes on the affine image and has positive character as an operation, so the exterior map stays strictly equivariant. The actual numerical value of \(\gamma\) still needs the genuine associative endomorphism model, its contraction, and four ordered insertions; ordinary vertex \((-1)\)-products alone do not evaluate it.
