# Ordered composition, duality and quantum states

Eliminating a field can change the law of every field that remains. Consider a free field on a half-space with a boundary current of level one. Insert two regulated Wilson rays with real charges \(a,-a\) at boundary positions \(r,0\), where \(0<r<1\). The current Ward equations determine the oscillator part of the outgoing neutral state. The mutual contraction of the rays supplies another datum, the scalar \(r^{-a^2}\). Now prepare a disk independently. Its holomorphic harmonic modes \(Z_n\) are independent centred complex Gaussians of variance \(n\). In the holomorphic Hilbert space it is represented, before normalization, by \(r^{-a^2}e^{a h(r)}\). Its squared norm is \(r^{-2a^2}(1-r^2)^{-a^2}\). For the disk's real collar with transfer parameter \(0<q\le1\), the normalized sewn value is \(((1-r^2)/(1-qr^2))^{a^2}\). The convergence of \(\sum r^{2n}/n\) is what permits this comparison of states at finite radius.

A second measurement in the same free theory detects information invisible to every single mode. Let \(p_n\) be the boundary creation variable corresponding to \(Z_n\).
