# Ordered composition, duality and quantum states

The programme has explicit field algebras, derived module comparisons, geometric operation systems and analytic sewings. Several are complete theories on their stated domains. The frontier includes further algebraic calculations and categorical classifications, as well as comparisons with independently defined bulk observables, defects and prepared states. Each comparison must preserve the operations that make its objects useful: multiplication, collision, exchange, units, source dependence and higher homotopies.

The Heisenberg current gives a small example of why the data matter. At nonzero level its double pole is a nonzero multiple of the vacuum. A unital map of fields to scalars would have to preserve that pole, while scalar fields have no singular product. The vacuum exists without a scalar augmentation. Elsewhere, the empty configuration, an occupied vacuum, a pointing and a physical cap are also distinct objects. A comparison that preserves one need not supply the others.

Its strong-cut restriction is a supported-section theorem; at three positions the relevant support is the nonconsecutive diagonal. This finite arity argument does not select a completion of modes or identify an independently defined continuous dual.
