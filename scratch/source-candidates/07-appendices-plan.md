## Glossary — rewritten core entries

Replace the corresponding WIP 0.06 entries; delete entries marked †.

- **Mass** — The AI-native operating system for the regulated life of a company. It creates a legal entity and then operates it — formation, identity, compliance, treasury, tax, governance, audit — continuously, with autonomous agents executing the work under human supervision, across many jurisdictions at once. Deployed by a government, it runs the regulated operations of the jurisdiction itself. *(Replaces "Mass (Modular Atomic State System)" — that expansion is retired.)*
- **Momentum** — The company that builds and operates special economic zones, using Mass as the operating system. It launches each zone with a portfolio of operating businesses and provides a managed service for governments that choose it.
- **Corridor** — A bilateral agreement between two jurisdictions specifying which of each other's verifications each will recognize, and at what grade. Asymmetric by design, revocable on its terms. What crosses a corridor is recognized standing; money and assets move through each jurisdiction's own lawful channels.
- **Recognized standing** — The portable proof of a company's regulated life: registration, licences, verified identity, compliance history, good standing — verifiable by any party a corridor entitles to rely on it.
- **Attestation** — A signed, tamper-evident record of an action executed on the substrate: who did what, when, under which rule, with which authority. Attestations accumulate into the continuous evidentiary stream that makes compliance a property of execution.
- **Multi-harbored company** — A company holding recognized standing in several jurisdictions at once, operated as one coherent entity, with the system applying the strictest applicable rule across all of its jurisdictions automatically.
- **Node** — A jurisdiction's sovereign deployment of the operating system: its own instance, on its own territory, under its own law, connected to the network by the corridors it chooses.
- **The five primitives** — Entities (who is acting), ownership (who owns what), fiscal instruments (how value is held and moved), identity (who is who), and consent (who has agreed to what): the universal elements every regulated economy must track, standardized in representation so jurisdictions can differ in everything else.
- **Living economy** — A zone that opens with operating businesses, employment, and transactions from its first day, via the portfolio method, rather than opening empty.
- **LICC — the Laboratory for Incentivization, Coordination and Cooperation** — The research institution this book proposes for the network: an independent laboratory turning the network's operating record into published, controlled evidence about what zone policy actually does. Framework published July 2025; constitution with academic partners is a project of the network's next phase. Its authority is limited to measurement and publication.
- † **Smart Asset** — retired with the October draft's vocabulary; the concepts it bundled are carried precisely by *recognized standing*, *attestation*, and *multi-harbored company*.
- † **Smart contract** (as used in governance entries) — replaced by *executable rules* within the rule layer.
