# On the form of meeting

The subject is what ordered things do when they meet. One wants to compose operators without losing their order, and to follow their singularities as their positions approach. One wants to fuse lines and understand what their ends do on a boundary. The need for a form is already present in the operations that must be performed.

The difficulty appears in a precise place. In the chiral operations of Beilinson and Drinfeld on a smooth curve over a characteristic-zero field, the inputs allow poles at collisions and the output lies on the common diagonal. Their chiral algebra is a Lie object. If a binary operation in this ordinary right differential-module domain is required to be strictly associative, both its triple composites vanish. Give it a local vacuum whose principal-part action is the identity, even on one side, and the whole module vanishes.

This obstruction clears a path by telling us what the form cannot hold. It also offers a temptation: to mistake the boundary of that form for the boundary of the subject. The Lie–commutative duality of Francis and Gaitsgory supplies the associative bar construction on Lie envelopes. It does not supply a general second associative chiral field algebra. The question of associative duality therefore remains a question in its own right.

A form expresses a content under particular conditions. It bears the limits of the question that called it forth. A successful definition need not become the definition of everything nearby. A complex on configurations, a family of fields and a category of representations make different things available to thought. Their relation must be constructed through the operations they carry. No choice of notation makes one of them absolute.

The inner content here is singular composition with order retained, together with the ways of separating, transporting and representing it. Geometry displays where meeting takes place. Fields display its coefficients. A category displays how objects act and combine. A physical theory supplies its observables, boundaries and defects; a prepared state supplies further data for evaluation. These forms stand beside one another. Their equivalence requires a particular map and its inverse.

A spoon and a sieve can both lift from a pot. One keeps the broth; the other lets it pass. Their shapes answer different tasks. Neither becomes a better utensil by taking the shape of the other.

There are two directions in the local picture. Along the complex curve, collision can be singular. Along a transverse real line, one composes in an order. The order of labels can thus remember a direction in space. For a smooth separated complex curve, the derived analytic functions on the ordered collision atlas agree with holomorphic-topological forms on configurations in the curve times the line.

This comparison respects permutations, deletion of labels and external products on separated configurations. Its scope is these function algebras. An identification of arbitrary coefficient systems, including their local units, asks for more. The order is therefore more than an ornament on a formula, while its interpretation as a physical direction still has a mathematical domain.

Positions can collide, and an ordered configuration can be cut into separated blocks. A residue records a collision. Here residue includes the principal part with every normal derivative; it is not merely the scalar coefficient that survives a contour integral. A cut exposes a coproduct: the object on the whole configuration maps to the objects on its blocks. Strong factorization says that this map is an equivalence on the appropriate disjoint locus.

Bar and cobar relate associative multiplication and coherent comultiplication. On the connected ordered coefficient categories, with scalar empty component and the specified augmentation or coaugmentation, they are inverse equivalences. At n labelled positions, a reduced word has length at most n. The comparison has a finite filtration there. No further global elementwise conilpotence assumption is needed. This does not yet identify the result with another physical field algebra.

Even the geometry has more than one useful form. The full atlas keeps all orders; a word construction keeps consecutive cuts. The natural projection from atlas to word need not preserve the tensor product by an equivalence. Already for two separated singleton inputs it can discard a nonzero summand. The word is useful because of what it retains, and its relation to the atlas includes what it forgets.

There are also two kinds of doing nothing. The scalar object supported only at the empty configuration is the unit for disjointness convolution. Tensoring at the same configuration uses the diagonal exceptional tensor, with the dualizing coefficient system as unit. The full-atlas cobar of this system is the unit of the transported tensor on atlas algebras. The word/cut cobar is a different vacuum algebra, the corresponding unit on the word carrier. On separated binary configurations the atlas cobar retains a nonzero coefficient where the cut cobar vanishes. A local vacuum marking carries information at nonempty configurations; the coaugmentation inserts only the empty unit.

A vacuum is not a scalar augmentation either. For the Heisenberg current with nonzero double-pole coefficient, that pole is a nonzero multiple of the vacuum. A unital map preserving field products would have to send it to the same nonzero scalar. Scalar fields have no singular products, so such a character does not exist. The identity field remains present.

The failure of a scalar splitting to preserve multiplication can be retained as curvature. For the free current the normalized dual curvature density is κcTc/2, where κ is the level, c is an odd ghost and T is translation. Within this paired vertex model, the central current pairing becomes a ghost state. The ordinary residue coalgebra constructed from these ghost fields with zero differential forgets the density, and hence the level. The distinction between emptiness and vacuum cannot be repaired by silently discarding this datum.

Now take the two descriptions as far apart as possible. At one pole remove the physical fields and retain only the vacuum and collision geometry. At the other begin with a field theory, its action and a boundary condition. The first isolates what the geometry itself requires. The second asks what an independently specified system actually does. Neither pole gains authority by reducing the other to an illustration.

On the half-space ℂ×ℝ≥0 take scalar superfields Φ,η of total degrees zero and one, the differential D=d_t+∂̄ and the quadratic action Sκ=τ(ηDΦ+κη∂_zη/2), where τ integrates against dz/(2πi). Impose i*η=0, with i the boundary inclusion, on the whole superfield. The exact Gaussian perturbative quantization has the universal Heisenberg algebra as its finite-energy boundary algebra. Normal-ordered products of derivatives of the current give the fields of all polynomial states. Bulk observables with separated normal support act on the retained boundary complex by continuous cochain operations.

Fix the quantum parameter to one and take the abelian level κ to be nonzero. A Wilson line can end at the boundary through a junction whose period comparison preserves current fields, rooted collision residues and local vacuum. This uses the rotation-finite junction sector and algebraic differential graded line modules. The separated comparison uses a coefficientwise Laurent completion; on bounded complexes of finite sums of scalar charge sectors it compares fusion.

A Gaussian transverse model with secondary pairing −1/κ has a strictly unital homotopy-coherent collision into its jet completion. After shuffle and symmetrization, its scalar bar twist agrees exactly with its line twist. Comparison of this monoid and its action with the native Heisenberg tower remains open.

In the abelian model with positive Schouten normalization, 1/κ appears three times. It is the degree-minus-two bracket of the surviving odd bulk class with itself. Multiplied by scalar charges q and q′, it is the residue of their line connection. The same pairing gives the exponent of the endpoint block, whose counterclockwise half-continuation has phase exp(πiqq′/κ). Here a transposition identifies the bracket with the line residue.

Transport of operations is the inverse transpose of state transport; on scalar charge sectors the phases are opposite. For unit charges at level three, an explicit graph transport along increasing normal coordinates has a closed-loop phase inverse and unequal to that of positive Fock continuation. A braided identification requires the corresponding reversal or another comparison. In the free case the forms can be laid side by side and compared. Their completions, orientations and receiving categories remain part of what is compared.

The equality of the poles is expressed by such correspondences. An order of labels can express a transverse direction. In the free-current coupling, the square of the ghost charge is minus the curvature’s zero mode. Reading this as the current two-point function evaluated on ghosts is a physical dictionary that enters no proof. A homotopy of products can be a residue. None asserts that all the surrounding categories have been identified.

Look now at the order 1, 2, 3. Habit makes the consecutive pairs easy to read: first 1 with 2, or first 2 with 3. The pair 1 with 3 looks misplaced. It can seem like a dash inserted where the sentence does not need one. But the middle position has not ceased to exist. The outer collision asks what happens across it.

In the reduced word/cut cobar of a strong cut coalgebra, the coefficient at n positions is a shifted local-cohomology object supported where every consecutive gap has an equality crossing it. At three positions that supporting locus is exactly the diagonal where the first and third coincide. The second may remain separate or may join them. That locus includes the triple diagonal.

After transporting their full principal parts to the same normal-contact module, the three routes in a compatible system of residues obey

L − R + N = 0.

Here L means that the first two meet first, R that the last two meet first, and N that the outer pair meets first and its result then meets the middle. Writing h for the outer residue, its differential in the two-term field complex is −N=L−R. This is the homotopy between the consecutive products. The residue complexes and their attaching maps carry it.

The same construction gives higher associative operations. For the first-cut transfer, the operation on n≥2 singleton inputs is the outermost residue, with suspension fixed. For general inputs in the field complexes, finite sums over trees remain. With nonidentity field exchange in a compatible residue system, reaching the outer partner requires every intervening exchange before taking the principal part. Associativity is therefore heard at three positions, and coherence continues beyond them. Two-point multiplication alone does not display this witness.

Return to the abstract pole and the scalar vacuum’s word/cut cobar. On a smooth separated geometrically connected curve over a field of characteristic zero, it has nonzero coefficients at several positions. At two points the reduced coefficient lies on the diagonal. At three it lies on the surface where the first and third coordinates agree. The identity fields have no singular operator product, yet the configuration coefficients retain principal parts and their residues.

At four positions the cut vacuum coefficient has a nonzero extension joining its cohomology modules. It is not their direct sum with shifts. Its product from two two-point coefficients, combined by cut convolution, is a nonzero derived morphism inducing zero on every cohomology module. The attachment and the product are distinct facts.

Even this bare object has operations that disappear when one keeps only its cohomology. The vacuum already requires a form capable of retaining a map that acts trivially on cohomology. Its apparent silence comes from the choice of what one listens to.

There is consequently no decision about form that can be made once for every case. There is a decision about what a given case needs. The input can be a Lie-algebra current, a Hopf algebra with a spectral form, a stress tensor or a matrix-valued field. In spectral constructions, compatible coactions belong to a covariant module alongside its fields.

Over k[c,(22+5c)⁻¹], with k a characteristic-zero field and c the central charge, the universal W₃ algebra has a curved quadratic vertex dual with noncommutative ghost fields. Its singular ghost product is nonzero, and its differential squares to the curvature’s zero mode. Nonlinear quadratic relations can require this different form.

Cyclic order can instead produce moments. For universal affine matrix currents at OPE scales κ/K and λ/K, normalized matrix-ordered vacuum traces have a finite expansion in even inverse powers of the rank K. Their planar limit gives noncrossing moments with cyclically ordered blocks. This construction supplies moments; identifying exchange or fusion requires a separate comparison.

On the word carrier, a geometric chiral bialgebra has a coherent coproduct for the transported tensor, with counit to the cut vacuum. A categorical chiral quantum group is a chiral tensor category with specified exchange; a geometric presentation must identify its representation system with that category. Spectral coproducts change level: spectral levels compose in order, and numerical translation levels add. For κ≠0, ordinary diagonal currents in two level-κ tensor factors have level 2κ. Such tensor products differ from fusion at fixed level κ.

Even a free current has two independent ways to change. Take its contraction to be κ/u² + λ/u. The even double pole carries the level. The odd simple pole changes field exchange: on two currents it subtracts 2λ/u times the tensor of the vacua. Exchange is the identity exactly when λ vanishes. In this polynomial family, contraction strength and odd exchange vary independently.

Kernels may live on the additive group, the multiplicative group or an elliptic curve, with their appropriate spectral rings. A function rational in a multiplicative coordinate need not be rational in an additive coordinate. The place where a pole lives belongs to the operation, just as its order does.

A deformation can move the poles themselves. For an even, nonnegatively integer-graded vertex algebra in characteristic zero, with translation raising degree by one and fields respecting the grading, the dilation twist gives a formal quantum vertex algebra. Old translation descendants are expressed by finite differences; a new translation gives ordinary derivative covariance. If degree zero consists only of the vacuum, an original nonzero homogeneous vacuum two-point block of pole order N becomes a rational expression with N simple poles spaced by the deformation parameter. These are poles of the rational block at nonzero parameter; the field algebra is constructed formally.

Dimension changes what can carry the level. For unshifted currents induced from a finite-dimensional Lie algebra on a smooth affine d-fold in characteristic zero, take the central summand to be the canonical differential module shifted by d−1. A central homotopy star-Lie operation of arity n and degree 2−n can occur only when n=d+1. The possible arity is binary on a curve and ternary on a surface; this does not assert existence for every Lie algebra.

In the charged-potential extension of the two-parameter free current, with both parameters and both charges nonzero, a specified charged two-point coefficient has infinitely many linearly independent branches around the puncture. It cannot be the value of a horizontal section of a finite-rank flat bundle under a single-valued meromorphic linear pairing. This obstruction includes irregular bundles.

Global support is another choice. On the total space of the trivial line plus the canonical line over a smooth connected projective complex curve, formal completion along the zero section kills a nonzero compactly supported perfect object on a displaced section, together with its identity. A sector has disappeared before any question of its continuation arises.

The vantage can change from boundary to bulk, from a line to a prepared state. One asks what collides, what acts, what ends and what is prepared. The algebra and its fusion do not by themselves select that preparation. A vacuum insertion, a scalar character and a physical state answer different questions.

The names of the forms can become walls. Bar–cobar duality, coefficient duality, a quadratic field dual, a line-direction bar construction and reflection of parameters are different operations. Internal Homs and endomorphisms of a chosen module add further constructions. Their inputs, variance and units must be named.

A centre must likewise be specified. The convolution centre and the scalar Hochschild centre solve different problems. Vacuum endomorphisms in a chosen category and a universal transverse actor are further objects. Identifying them with independent bulk observables requires comparison. A matching character or scalar residue is insufficient when the next composition uses data that this match has forgotten.

Cohomology can be just such an incomplete image. On the finite Chevalley–Eilenberg complex of a finite-dimensional semisimple Lie algebra in characteristic zero, the degree-minus-two bracket from a nondegenerate shifted invariant form induces zero on cohomology. On cochains it retains the inverse of that form, obtained from the current level by adding half the Killing form. The boundary current products still contain the level.

The affine cochain complex has operations beyond its constant ghosts. For finite-dimensional Lie algebras in characteristic zero and any invariant symmetric level, affine cochains yield a unital transverse monoid acting on affine vacuum coefficients. Its carrier uses joint formal zero modes, polynomial positive-energy states and the algebraic sum over energies. With invertible shifted form, the closed graded-skew degree-minus-two secondary bracket on all universal polynomial affine Chevalley–Eilenberg states has coherent Jacobi homotopies. Lifting this state structure to configuration-indexed geometric operations, with all mixed product and action coherences, remains open.

A comparison is adequate when it carries the operations its purpose consumes. For ordered collisions this includes residues and cuts, the empty unit and local vacuum, the outer homotopy and normal derivatives. One must determine what a forgetful map loses before using its image to identify objects. Then one must carry the whole diagram. The answer can be exact for this case without becoming a prescription for every other case.

There are forms whose comparison is still open. Beyond the constructed quadratic field duals, a general dual with fields and compatible extensions across collisions remains to be constructed. The function-level comparison between ordered configurations on a curve and configurations in the curve times a real line has not become a general comparison of their enhanced coefficient objects with units.

For currents of a finite-dimensional nonabelian Lie algebra in characteristic zero, with invertible shifted form β=κ+Kil/2, its inverse gives the Casimir residue on finite-dimensional charge tops in the Wilson quotient where ghosts and positive modes vanish. The boundary modules are affine Weyl modules induced from these tops. The normalized comparison among the moving affine centralizer, the restricted bar construction in the line direction and line fusion remains open, with its weight and length completions. It must preserve all induced states, nonlinear translation homotopies, native products and three-module coherence.

There remains the joint holographic comparison with an independently defined nonabelian bulk: to prove that its chosen lines are modules of the specified dual, with their endpoints, coupling and fusion, and to identify its local operations on the same terms. Preparing a state from an independently defined gravitational bulk, with regulated amplitudes and sewing, is a further open problem. Each asks for the form in which its still unperformed operation can be made exact.
