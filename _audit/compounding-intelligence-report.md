## Argument and reader payoff

Institutions can retain experience so a successor can retrieve comparable cases and assess rule proposals. That usefulness also makes the choice and ranking of evidence consequential. The essay carries the reader from retained cases through rule authority to manipulation of attention and the need to question the selected record.
